# Tape

A torn strip of masking tape that holds a window or card down at a corner or across the top, as if it was stuck back onto the sheet by hand.

- **Provide:** `pb-taped` on the host, then one or two `<span class="pb-tape pb-tape--tl" aria-hidden="true">` inside it. Positions: `--tl`, `--tr`, `--bl`, `--br`, `--top`.
- **Do:** use one or two strips per piece, on opposite corners. Mix positions across a page so no two pieces look the same.
- **Don't:** tape every card, or let tape cover text or the important part of an image.
