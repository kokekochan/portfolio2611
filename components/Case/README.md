# Case

A rebuilt housing around a screen: a thick `panel` bezel, a vent grille and a label-maker strip on the bottom plate.

- **Provide:** `<div class="pb-case">` holding one `.pb-window` (usually `pb-term pb-crt`) and a `.pb-case__plate` with a `.pb-case__vent` and a `.pb-dymo`.
- **Do:** use it for the lead project's terminal, once per page. The window inside loses its own shadow; the case carries it.
- **Don't:** nest cases, or put the case around artwork.
- **Label:** the plate names the piece (`Fig. 01`) or the section, never my name.
- **No screws or fake hardware.** The case is plain.
