# Stray

Oversized stray type from the section itself, sitting behind the content and crossing its edges: a giant outlined number tilted off the grid, or the section's word running down the side in mono.

- **Provide:** `pb-stray-host` on the section, then `<span class="pb-stray" aria-hidden="true">06</span>` (outlined in `second-ink`) or `<span class="pb-stray pb-stray--v" aria-hidden="true">Code</span>` (vertical, outlined in `rule`). Place it with `--x`, `--t` and `--r`.
- **Do:** take the text from the section: its first Fig. number, its name, a technique listed in it. One stray per section at most, and not in every section.
- **Don't:** use my name, put it behind running text, or let it reach readable contrast; it is an outline only.
