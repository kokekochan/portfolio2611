# Xerox

Photocopy wear for a Paper section: toner speckle over the whole sheet and dark bands down both edges where the copier lid did not close. With it, `pb-torn` gives a paper card a torn bottom edge.

- **Provide:** `pb-xerox` on a `data-theme="paper"` section (it replaces `pb-grain` there; don't use both). `pb-torn` on a `pb-card`.
- **Do:** keep it on Paper only. Speckle stays light enough that captions stay readable; check any new caption colour against it.
- **Don't:** use it on the Ink page or inside screens; screens wear out differently (`CRT`).
